I am an engineering student at CentraleSupélec, on a gap year as an AI/ML engineer at Dassault Systèmes K.K. in Tokyo. I work at both ends of a problem: the mathematics that says what a method can and cannot do, and the engineering that makes it run on real hardware for real users.

It started in Mali in 2024: designing an offline voice assistant for farmers ([IAgriM](#/experience/iagrim)), I saw what an idea paired with AI could change where nothing else reached. I joined CentraleSupélec's entrepreneurship track, and [Autogear](#/projects/autogear) soon turned from a venture into R&D: an embedded model standing in for a costly torque sensor. With the CEA-DAM, Bayesian active learning [routed an explosive device](#/projects/blast-routing) with 19 simulations out of 10,000 candidate positions. A [critical reading of a tensor-network paper](#/research/vtnn-notes), and the discussions with Zeno Toffano that followed, made physics-inspired machine learning the field I want to pursue.

Before CentraleSupélec, I spent two years in preparatory classes at Collège Stanislas, near-pure theory in mathematics and physics; I later returned there as an oral examiner in mathematics.
