Outside work, I love music, cinema, art and design. I produce electronic music on Ableton, and a good album on repeat or a film that stays with me for days is where I recharge. Art caught me early: in 2016, I was a performer in a work by Tino Sehgal at the Palais de Tokyo. Design follows me everywhere, from posters and music covers to visuals for student events and the small details of this site. I also practise martial arts: judo, karate, kendo and iaido.
