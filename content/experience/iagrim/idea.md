Danew Talla Electronics tasked me with narrowing the digital divide with existing technology. I saw the gap as one of access, not knowledge: Malian farmers have daily questions about their work, and the agronomic answers exist, but three obstacles keep them out of reach: poor connectivity, local languages that are mostly spoken rather than written, and low digital literacy. These are major obstacles to Mali's agricultural growth. IAgriM is an assistant designed to answer those questions despite all three.
