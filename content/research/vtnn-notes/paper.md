Saeed S. Jahromi and Román Orús (DIPC, Multiverse Computing) replace the dense layers of a neural network by **matrix product operators**, a tensor network from many-body quantum physics, and train them with a variant of **DMRG**, the density-matrix renormalisation group: instead of one global gradient step, training sweeps through pairs of neighbouring tensors and updates each pair locally. The abstract puts forward scalability; the benchmarks cover linear and non-linear regression, the classification of 2D point clouds, and MNIST.
