*Claim.* The algorithm gives insight into the entanglement structure of the weights and the correlations between parameters.

*Reading.* Because the SVD is part of every update, the entropy at each bond is available throughout training, not computed afterwards by a separate probe. On Gaussian blobs, accuracy reaches 99 % and the entropy collapses to almost zero; on three intertwined spirals, it settles near 0.7–0.8. Entanglement had already served to read tensor-network models, but those were pure tensor networks, or architectures rather than trained weights.

*Verdict.* The claim holds, and it is the paper's strongest result: a new dimension of interpretability for neural networks, opened by a new combination of structure and training.
