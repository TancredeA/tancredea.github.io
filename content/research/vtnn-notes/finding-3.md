*Claim.* The algorithm gives insight into the entanglement structure of the weights and the correlations between parameters.

*Reading.* On Gaussian blobs, accuracy reaches 99 % and the entropy collapses to almost zero for every bond dimension. On three intertwined spirals, the loss still falls, but the entropy settles on a plateau near 0.7–0.8: the weights stay entangled, as expected for data that no product-state model can separate.

*Verdict.* The claim holds, and it is the paper's strongest result: well-separated data, low entropy; intertwined data, high entropy. A standard dense network offers no such reading.
