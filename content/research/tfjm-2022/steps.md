With Luigi de Alfaro, as part of team SLG, we went through the whole cycle:

1. **Choose a problem.** Out of the eight problems published for 2022, we took Problem 3, "The Confined Professor".
2. **Five months of research.** Our answers were not found in one go: we put forward strategies, refined them question after question, and went back to earlier answers when a later question taught us something.
3. **Write it up.** We wrote our results down with their proofs: an original report in French, later adapted into English.
4. **Defend it.** At the regional tournament, each team defends its own solution, opposes another team's and reports on a debate. We placed 3rd.
