The TFJM² (Tournoi Français des Jeunes Mathématiciennes et Mathématiciens) is a research tournament for high-school students, run by Animath since 2011. Every December–January it publishes eight problems drawn from research work, with no complete solution known to the jury. Teams of four to six students work on them for several months, in the position of researchers, then defend their work over a weekend in April, in regional tournaments, followed by a national final.
