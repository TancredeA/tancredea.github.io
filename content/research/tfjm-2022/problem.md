Timothé (T) falls ill and asks his colleague Perrine (P) to take over his class for a week. The class has n students and the room has n seats. Perrine seats the students as she wishes. When Timothé returns, he wants to recover the seating plan chosen by Perrine, by asking her questions which she answers at the end of the week, and he wants the list of questions to be as short as possible.

The quality of a strategy is measured by its **worst case**: the maximum number of questions needed to be certain of the plan, whatever P answers.
