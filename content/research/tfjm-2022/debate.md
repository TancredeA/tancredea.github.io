Each round is a debate on one problem, in which teams take turns in three roles:

- **defender**: presents the main ideas and results the team obtained;
- **opponent**: analyses the defender's solution and talk critically, pointing out errors and imprecisions as well as strengths;
- **rapporteur**: assesses the debate between the two, and must spot what the opponent missed.

Before each round, the opponent and the rapporteur hand the jury a one-to-two-page note on the defender's solution.
