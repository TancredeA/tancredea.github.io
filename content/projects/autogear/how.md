Once per second, the box turns its sensors into 14 inputs: the speed, derived from the cadence and the engaged gear, the slope, the acceleration, coasting, and their means and trends over 3 to 15 s. 100 boosted trees estimate the development (metres per crank turn) of the gear the rider will want 3 s ahead. A hysteresis controller shifts one step when that target has stayed beyond the neighbouring gear for 3 s, and never while coasting. The closed loop runs through the controller only.
