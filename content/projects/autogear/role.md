- **Embedded electronics** (with Vincent Lucien, during the project): soldering and wiring, the Arduino code that reads the sensors and drives the servo, assembling the prototype and fitting the components into its boxes.
- **AI pipeline** (during the project): framing gear shifting as learning from demonstration under partial observation, the design of the dataset the prototype was to record (rides labelled with a reference rider's shifts, never recorded in the end), feature engineering, the two-stage shift classifier and the benchmark of supervised models (XGBoost, random forest, multinomial logistic regression, SVM) within the board's memory and latency limits.
- **The extension** (solo, after the project, Oct 2026): rebuilding the dataset from public rides, the closed-loop replay protocol, the reformulation as a target-gear regression with a hysteresis controller, the comparison with decision tables and other models, and the C99 firmware library, verified against Python.
