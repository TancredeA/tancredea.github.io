- **Machine intelligence, from start to finish.** I led the AI side of the project: framing gear shifting as learning from demonstration under partial observation, the design of the dataset, the features, and the benchmark of supervised models (XGBoost, random forest, multinomial logistic regression, SVM) within the board's memory and latency limits. In June 2026, I delivered a first model to close Autogear as an academic project.
- **The model that works.** Over the summer and until October 2026, I came back to my own model to improve it: I rebuilt the data from public rides, judged every policy in closed-loop replay, replaced the shift classifier with a target-gear regression and a hysteresis controller, and wrote the C99 library that runs it on the board's microcontroller, verified against Python.
- **Embedded electronics** (with Vincent Lucien): soldering and wiring, the Arduino code that reads the sensors and drives the servo, assembling the prototype and fitting the components into its boxes.
