- **Everything but the data.** The research side was done during the project: the search and comparison of models, the features, the design of the dataset and the benchmark protocol. The prototype itself was modified to record rides, its sensors and the rider's shifts logged together. Time ran out before the recording campaign could be run.
- **A reconstruction.** To show what that training would have produced, I rebuilt the pipeline on public data: rides from an open database (cadence, speed, altitude), adapted to our setting, with the gear recovered from the ratio of speed to cadence and the shifts labelled where it jumps. Same features, same two-stage models, same protocol.
