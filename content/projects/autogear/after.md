- **Everything but the data.** During the project, the team designed the dataset, the features, the benchmark protocol and the two-stage classifier, and modified the prototype to record rides. Time ran out before the recording campaign could be run.
- **The extension.** After the project, on my own, I rebuilt that dataset from public rides, judged every policy in closed-loop replay, and found that the planned classifier fell short. Predicting the target gear instead agrees with the rider within one gear 87.0 % of the time, against 80.8 % for a decision table given the torque and 66.0 % for the planned classifier, in closed-loop simulation on reconstructed public data.
- **Other models, same target.** A small MLP reaches 86.8 % (2.2 KB), a random forest 85.8 % (31.4 KB), a linear regression 84.8 % (172 B), a learned speed × slope table 82.0 % (289 B). The boosted trees were kept for their consistency (13 wins of 14), their behaviour on short training sets and their simple deployment.
- **The report.** Ten pages in IEEE format, below: the data reconstruction, the protocol, why the first approach falls short, the results and the firmware. The code, the data pipeline and the firmware library will be published on GitHub.
