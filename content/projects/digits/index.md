## Why tensor networks

Matrix product states compress a function of many variables into a chain of small tensors. Stoudenmire and Schwab (2016) showed that such a chain can serve as a classifier for handwritten digits. I am rebuilding that idea to understand where it competes with a neural network and where it does not.
