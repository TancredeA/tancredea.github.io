*Problem.* The optimized wall is computed from the energy the source puts into the hall, so it is only as realistic as the excitation. A single isotropic note is enough to test the loop, not to design a wall.

*Choice.* Nine spectral lines (a C-major chord and two harmonics per note, 260 Hz to 1.2 kHz), each a narrow Gaussian in frequency, injected along the stage through a Gaussian profile whose width shrinks as frequency rises, a proxy for directivity.

*Why.* Instruments radiate more directionally at high frequency. Rerunning with a C-minor chord gave energy peaks at the same frequencies, which suggests the optimum is not tied to this particular chord.
