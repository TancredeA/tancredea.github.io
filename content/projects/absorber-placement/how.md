A 44 m × 28 m slice of a shoebox hall, modeled on the Concertgebouw, is driven by a source on the stage. Each iteration solves the direct problem for the pressure p and the adjoint problem for q, which together give the gradient; a projected descent then moves the material. The team ran this loop over several frequencies and fixed the design one parameter at a time: mineral wool, 50 % coverage, then a level-2 pre-fractal wall (level 3 is hard to build for a marginal gain).
