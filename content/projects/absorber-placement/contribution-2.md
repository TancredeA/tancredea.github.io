*Problem.* In the weak form, the term −k²∫|p|² destroys coercivity, so Lax–Milgram does not apply.

*Choice.* Lift the stage datum, write the operator as an invertible part plus a compact one, and apply the Fredholm alternative: existence then reduces to uniqueness, which follows from the energy identity and unique continuation.

*Why.* Uniqueness needs the material to dissipate (Im α ≠ 0) on an open piece Σ of the wall. Without it, the hall becomes a rigid cavity that resonates at its eigenfrequencies, and the stability constant blows up nearby, consistent with the energy peaks observed against frequency.
