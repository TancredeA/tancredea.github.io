*Problem.* The same material could line an open-air venue or an engine duct, where energy leaves through the sides.

*Choice.* Replace the rigid side walls by the first-order absorbing condition ∂ₙp = ik p.

*Why.* The new boundary terms do not depend on χ, so the gradient keeps the same expression. Radiation alone now gives uniqueness, without assuming χ > 0 anywhere, provided the material is dissipative or neutral.
