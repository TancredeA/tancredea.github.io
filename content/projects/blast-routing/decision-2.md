*Problem.* Simulating all 2,500 positions of a 3D scene is out of reach.

*Choice.* A Gaussian process learns only the gap between ARMEN and the Kingery–Bulmash free-field law.

*Why.* The residual carries only confinement and diffraction effects, so it is smoother than the full field and the GP converges in a few runs. The GP also returns its own uncertainty, which the acquisition needs.
