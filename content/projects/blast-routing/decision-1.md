*Problem.* A route can be judged by the worst blast it risks, by its average danger, or by the danger it accumulates.

*Choice.* Cumulative damage, plus a turn penalty for the vehicle.

*Why.* If detonation is a rare event with constant rate λ per meter, expected damage reduces to the integral of danger along the route. The criterion is derived rather than postulated, and it already penalizes length; an average would ignore exposure time.
